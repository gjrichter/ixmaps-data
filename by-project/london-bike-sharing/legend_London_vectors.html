Bike Sharing Trip Visualization

The visualization shows the main bike trips of a period of a day. You can change the period or the day with the selection boxes in the upper left.

Yellow arrows represent the accumulated trip count between 2 stations. Arrows are shown only if we have at least 3 trips.

Circles show trips with the same start and end station.
