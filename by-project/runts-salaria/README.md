# runts-salaria

Entities of the Third Sector registered in the RUNTS (Registro Unico Nazionale del Terzo Settore) along the Via Salaria (provinces of Roma, Rieti, Ascoli Piceno) and in the 138 comuni of the 2016 earthquake crater.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `runts_iscritti_salaria_cratere_20261016.csv` | 12,887 registered entities from the RUNTS list "Elenco enti iscritti" of 2026-10-16 (file `20261016_iscritti_v1.1.xlsx`): every entity of the provinces RM, RI, AP, plus those of the crater comuni in the other provinces (MC, TE, PG, AN, FM, AQ, TR, PE; comuni list: `../sisma-2016-cratere/cratere_sisma_2016.geojson`, matched by name and province); columns: tax code, repertoire, name, section, network, comune, province, 5x1000, registration date. The legal representative's name column of the source was left out |
| `salaria_trace.geojson` | trace of the Via Salaria |

## Sources
