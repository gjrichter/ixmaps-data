# runts-salaria

Entities of the Third Sector registered in the RUNTS (Registro Unico Nazionale del Terzo Settore) in the provinces of Roma, Rieti and Ascoli Piceno along the Via Salaria.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `runts_iscritti_rm_ri_ap_20261016.csv` | 11,433 registered entities (province RM, RI, AP) from the RUNTS list "Elenco enti iscritti" of 2026-10-16 (file `20261016_iscritti_v1.1.xlsx`); columns: tax code, repertoire, name, section, network, comune, province, 5x1000, registration date. The legal representative's name column of the source was left out |
| `salaria_trace.geojson` | trace of the Via Salaria |

## Sources
- RUNTS, Ministero del Lavoro e delle Politiche Sociali, https://servizi.lavoro.gov.it/runts/it-it/Lista-enti

CDN: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/gjrichter/ixmaps-data@main/by-project/runts-salaria/
